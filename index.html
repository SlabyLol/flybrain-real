Interaction Guide - Real Connectome
Feed -- Click on the canvas to place food. The fly will seek and eat it when hungry.
Touch -- Click on the fly to touch it. Different body parts trigger different responses.
Air -- Click and drag near the fly to blow wind.
Light / Temp -- Cycle conditions. The fly shows phototaxis and temperature preferences.
Brain 3D -- Toggle 3D brain visualization of neuron activity.

This uses the real FlyWire connectome (139k neurons). Behavior emerges from the wiring.
